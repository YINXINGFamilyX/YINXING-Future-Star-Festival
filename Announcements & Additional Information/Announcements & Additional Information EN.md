## FAMILYX ESPORTS YINXING FUTURE STAR FESTIVAL

<sub>*FAMILYX ESPORTS YINXING FUTURE STAR FESTIVAL is a Counter-Strike 2 Tier 2 Ranked Tournament organized and operated by FAMILYX ESPORTS for eligible teams and players from China. All eight teams in the Offline Playoffs will qualify through two Open Qualifiers before competing at the LAN stage in Jin'an District, Fuzhou, Fujian, China.*</sub>

## Team Registration & Eligibility

- The tournament is open to eligible teams and players from China
- The tournament does not feature VRS direct invites, Tournament Operator direct invites or wildcard invites
- Every team competing in the Offline Playoffs must qualify through an Open Qualifier
- Registration is free of charge through the tournament functionality on 5EPlay. FAMILYX ESPORTS does not charge a tournament registration fee
- Each Open Qualifier may feature up to 64 teams
- Teams that fail to qualify through Open Qualifier #1 may participate in Open Qualifier #2 free of charge
- Teams must complete team registration, player registration, roster confirmation, account verification and eligibility review as required
- A registered roster may include five starting players, one substitute player and one coach
- All participants must compete using their own registered accounts, and no participant may simultaneously hold a competition-related role for two different teams

All participating teams, players, substitute players, coaches and relevant team personnel must comply with the official tournament rules and principles of competitive integrity.
