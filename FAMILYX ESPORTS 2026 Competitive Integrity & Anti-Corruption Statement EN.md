## 1. PURPOSE AND FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES

FAMILYX ESPORTS AND ITS CORPORATE OPERATING ENTITY, 天津爱勾勒文化传播有限公司, CONSIDER COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY, FAIR COMPETITION AND PUBLIC TRUST TO BE FUNDAMENTAL AND NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES OF ESPORTS TOURNAMENT OPERATIONS.

FAMILYX ESPORTS MAINTAINS ZERO TOLERANCE TOWARD MATCH-FIXING, MATCH MANIPULATION, BETTING-RELATED CORRUPTION, BRIBERY, MISUSE OF INSIDE INFORMATION, COLLUSION AND ANY OTHER CONDUCT THAT UNDERMINES OR MAY UNDERMINE COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY.

NO PERSON MAY DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY IMPROPERLY INFLUENCE A MATCH RESULT, THE PROGRESS OF A MATCH, PLAYER PERFORMANCE, MATCH STATISTICS OR ANY OTHER MATTER CAPABLE OF AFFECTING THE FAIRNESS OF COMPETITION.

FAMILYX ESPORTS WILL ADDRESS COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY MATTERS ON THE BASIS OF FACTS AND EVIDENCE, AND IN ACCORDANCE WITH PRINCIPLES OF FAIRNESS, CARE, NECESSITY AND PROPORTIONALITY.

THIS STATEMENT APPLIES TO ALL ESPORTS TITLES AND COMPETITIONS ORGANIZED, OPERATED OR ADMINISTERED BY FAMILYX ESPORTS DURING 2026 AND IS NOT LIMITED TO ANY SINGLE GAME TITLE OR EVENT.

## 4. PROHIBITION OF MATCH-FIXING, MATCH MANIPULATION AND CORRUPT CONDUCT

NO PERSON SUBJECT TO THIS STATEMENT MAY INTENTIONALLY COMMIT, ATTEMPT, FACILITATE, INSTRUCT, ARRANGE OR COLLUDE WITH ANOTHER PERSON TO COMMIT MATCH MANIPULATION.

PROHIBITED CONDUCT INCLUDES, BUT IS NOT LIMITED TO:

1. INTENTIONALLY LOSING A MATCH;

2. INTENTIONALLY LOSING, CONCEDING OR IMPROPERLY PLAYING A PARTICULAR MAP, ROUND, STAGE OR OTHER PART OF A COMPETITION;

3. INTENTIONALLY CONTROLLING, ALTERING OR INFLUENCING A FINAL OR INTERMEDIATE SCORE;

4. DELIBERATELY REDUCING AN INDIVIDUAL'S OR TEAM'S COMPETITIVE PERFORMANCE WITHOUT A LEGITIMATE COMPETITIVE REASON;

5. DELIBERATELY MANIPULATING KILLS, DEATHS, ASSISTS, ECONOMY STATISTICS OR OTHER MATCH STATISTICS;

6. DELIBERATELY MANIPULATING FIRST KILLS, PISTOL ROUNDS, MAP RESULTS, OVERTIME RESULTS OR OTHER SEPARATELY MEASURABLE OR PREDICTABLE MATCH EVENTS;

7. PRE-ARRANGING A MATCH RESULT, MATCH PROGRESSION OR SPECIFIC MATCH EVENT WITH AN OPPONENT, ANOTHER TEAM, STAFF MEMBER OR THIRD PARTY;

8. ALTERING NORMAL COMPETITIVE BEHAVIOR IN RESPONSE TO BETTING ACTIVITY, ODDS, BETTING MARKETS, FINANCIAL INCENTIVES OR OTHER EXTERNAL BENEFITS;

9. SOLICITING, ACCEPTING, OFFERING, PROVIDING, PROMISING OR ARRANGING MONEY, PROPERTY, SERVICES, BENEFITS OR ANY OTHER FORM OF CONSIDERATION TO INFLUENCE A COMPETITION;

10. USING INSIDE OR NON-PUBLIC INFORMATION TO ASSIST ANOTHER PERSON IN OBTAINING A BETTING OR OTHER IMPROPER BENEFIT;

11. CONCEALING, ASSISTING IN THE CONCEALMENT OF, OR OTHERWISE FACILITATING MATCH MANIPULATION OR RELATED CORRUPT CONDUCT;

12. OTHERWISE INTENTIONALLY UNDERMINING THE AUTHENTICITY, FAIRNESS, INDEPENDENCE OR COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY OF A MATCH.

AN ATTEMPT, ARRANGEMENT, ACT OF FACILITATION, INSTRUCTION OR COLLUSION MAY CONSTITUTE A SERIOUS COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY VIOLATION EVEN WHERE THE INTENDED MANIPULATION DOES NOT ULTIMATELY CHANGE THE RESULT OF A MATCH.

## 5. BETTING RESTRICTIONS

PLAYERS, SUBSTITUTE PLAYERS, COACHES, MANAGERS, TEAM LEADERS AND OTHER TEAM MEMBERS CAPABLE OF DIRECTLY INFLUENCING COMPETITIVE PERFORMANCE IN A FAMILYX ESPORTS EVENT MUST NOT DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY BET ON PROFESSIONAL ESPORTS COMPETITIONS IN THE GAME TITLE IN WHICH THEY PARTICIPATE.

FOR EXAMPLE, PERSONS PARTICIPATING IN COUNTER-STRIKE 2 COMPETITIONS MUST NOT DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY BET ON ANY PROFESSIONAL COUNTER-STRIKE 2 COMPETITION.

THE FOLLOWING METHODS MAY NOT BE USED TO CIRCUMVENT THIS PROHIBITION:

* THIRD-PARTY ACCOUNTS;
* ACCOUNTS BELONGING TO FRIENDS OR FAMILY MEMBERS;
* INSTRUCTING ANOTHER PERSON TO PLACE A BET;
* PROVIDING FUNDS FOR A BET;
* SHARING THE COST OF A BET;
* SHARING BETTING PROFITS;
* USING AN AGENT OR OTHER INTERMEDIARY;
* OTHERWISE PARTICIPATING IN OR EXERCISING CONTROL OVER BETTING ACTIVITY.

FAMILYX ESPORTS STAFF MUST NOT DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY BET ON:

* A COMPETITION ORGANIZED, OPERATED OR ADMINISTERED BY FAMILYX ESPORTS;
* A COMPETITION IN WHICH THEY ARE CURRENTLY PERFORMING TOURNAMENT MANAGEMENT, ADMINISTRATION, REFEREEING, PRODUCTION OR OTHER DUTIES;
* A COMPETITION FOR WHICH THEY POSSESS MATERIAL NON-PUBLIC INFORMATION BY REASON OF THEIR ROLE.

* OTHER INFORMATION CAPABLE OF MATERIALLY AFFECTING COMPETITIVE JUDGMENT, BETTING MARKETS OR THIRD-PARTY DECISION-MAKING.

NO PERSON MAY DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY PROVIDE SUCH INFORMATION TO A PERSON NOT ENTITLED TO RECEIVE IT FOR THE PURPOSE OF BETTING, FINANCIAL GAIN, AN UNFAIR COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE OR ANY OTHER IMPROPER PURPOSE.

## 7. STAFF OBLIGATIONS AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

FAMILYX ESPORTS STAFF MUST GIVE PRIORITY TO THE FAIRNESS AND COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY OF THE EVENT AND MUST NOT USE THEIR ROLE, ACCESS OR INFORMATIONAL ADVANTAGE TO:

* IMPROPERLY INFLUENCE A MATCH;
* PROVIDE AN UNFAIR ADVANTAGE TO ANY TEAM OR PERSON;
* DISCLOSE NON-PUBLIC TOURNAMENT INFORMATION;
* PARTICIPATE IN IMPROPER FINANCIAL ARRANGEMENTS RELATED TO A MATCH;
* FACILITATE OR CONCEAL MATCH-FIXING OR MATCH MANIPULATION;
* USE INSIDE INFORMATION FOR BETTING;
* ASSIST ANOTHER PERSON IN USING INSIDE INFORMATION FOR BETTING OR OTHER IMPROPER BENEFIT.

WHERE A STAFF MEMBER, A PERSON WITH A DIRECT FINANCIAL INTEREST CONNECTED TO THAT STAFF MEMBER, OR ANOTHER DUTY PERFORMED BY THAT STAFF MEMBER CREATES AN ACTUAL OR POTENTIAL CONFLICT OF INTEREST WITH A FAMILYX ESPORTS EVENT, THE STAFF MEMBER SHOULD PROMPTLY DISCLOSE THE MATTER TO FAMILYX ESPORTS.

FAMILYX ESPORTS MAY IMPLEMENT REASONABLE INFORMATION ACCESS RESTRICTIONS, SEPARATION OF RESPONSIBILITIES, RECUSAL REQUIREMENTS OR OTHER CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST CONTROLS WHERE APPROPRIATE.

## 10. INVESTIGATION AND EVIDENCE PRINCIPLES

UPON RECEIVING A REPORT, ANOMALY, DATA IRREGULARITY OR OTHER COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY INDICATOR, FAMILYX ESPORTS MAY CONDUCT A PRELIMINARY REVIEW AND MAY COMMENCE A FORMAL INVESTIGATION WHERE REASONABLE GROUNDS EXIST.

INVESTIGATIVE MATERIAL MAY INCLUDE, BUT IS NOT LIMITED TO:

* MATCH DEMOS;
* SERVER LOGS;
* ANTI-CHEAT RECORDS;
* MATCH RECORDINGS;
* TOURNAMENT VOICE COMMUNICATION RECORDINGS;
* RELEVANT TOURNAMENT PLATFORM RECORDS;
* MATCH STATISTICS;
* STATEMENTS FROM RELEVANT PERSONS;
* COMMUNICATION RECORDS OR OTHER MATERIAL OBTAINED WITHIN A LAWFUL AND PROPER SCOPE;
* OTHER EVIDENCE REASONABLY CAPABLE OF ASSISTING FACTUAL DETERMINATION.

FAMILYX ESPORTS MAY REQUIRE RELEVANT PERSONS TO PROVIDE A WRITTEN EXPLANATION, PARTICIPATE IN NECESSARY QUESTIONING, PROVIDE TOURNAMENT-RELATED FILES OR DATA, OR COOPERATE WITH REASONABLE COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY CHECKS.

THE SCOPE OF AN INVESTIGATION SHOULD BE REASONABLY CONNECTED TO THE MATTER UNDER REVIEW AND SHOULD, WHERE PRACTICABLE, FOLLOW A PRINCIPLE OF NECESSITY.

REFUSING A REASONABLE INVESTIGATIVE REQUEST, INTENTIONALLY DELETING OR DESTROYING RELEVANT EVIDENCE, ALTERING EVIDENCE, PROVIDING FALSE MATERIAL, KNOWINGLY CONCEALING MATERIAL FACTS OR OTHERWISE OBSTRUCTING AN INVESTIGATION MAY CONSTITUTE A SEPARATE VIOLATION.

---

## 11. ALLEGATIONS, ANOMALIES AND FINDINGS

A REPORT, SOCIAL MEDIA ALLEGATION, INDUSTRY RUMOR, UNUSUAL ODDS, UNUSUAL BETTING ACTIVITY, STATISTICAL ANOMALY OR OTHER INFORMATION THAT HAS NOT BEEN FULLY VERIFIED DOES NOT, BY ITSELF, ESTABLISH THAT A COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY VIOLATION HAS OCCURRED.

**AN ALLEGATION IS NOT A FINAL FINDING.**

ANY COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY DETERMINATION SHOULD BE MADE AFTER CONSIDERATION OF THE RELEVANT FACTS, EVIDENCE AND THE PARTICULAR COMPETITIVE CONTEXT.

A SINGLE STATISTICAL ANOMALY, SINGLE UNUSUAL PERFORMANCE OR UNVERIFIED THIRD-PARTY ASSESSMENT SHOULD NOT, IN THE ABSENCE OF OTHER RELIABLE EVIDENCE, AUTOMATICALLY BE TREATED AS FINAL PROOF OF MATCH-FIXING OR MATCH MANIPULATION.

## 12. INTERIM PROTECTIVE MEASURES

WHERE A REALISTIC RISK EXISTS TO A MATCH, AN INVESTIGATION, PRESERVATION OF EVIDENCE OR THE INTEGRITY OF A COMPETITION, FAMILYX ESPORTS MAY TAKE NECESSARY INTERIM PROTECTIVE MEASURES PENDING REVIEW, INCLUDING, BUT NOT LIMITED TO:

* TEMPORARILY SUSPENDING A PARTICIPANT;
* TEMPORARILY SUSPENDING A STAFF MEMBER FROM DUTIES;
* TEMPORARILY WITHHOLDING RELEVANT PRIZE OR REWARD PAYMENTS;
* PAUSING OR ADJUSTING A MATCH;
* PRESERVING SERVER, VOICE AND TOURNAMENT DATA;
* TEMPORARILY RESTRICTING ACCESS TO SYSTEMS, VENUES OR INFORMATION;
* TAKING OTHER REASONABLE MEASURES NECESSARY TO PROTECT THE INVESTIGATION OR FAIRNESS OF THE COMPETITION.

**AN INTERIM MEASURE DOES NOT CONSTITUTE A FINAL FINDING THAT A PERSON OR TEAM HAS COMMITTED A VIOLATION.**

WHERE AN INVESTIGATION DOES NOT PRODUCE SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE TO SUPPORT A VIOLATION, FAMILYX ESPORTS SHOULD PROMPTLY REMOVE INTERIM MEASURES THAT ARE NO LONGER NECESSARY.
